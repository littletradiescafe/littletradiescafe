Our Story

A cafe built on a building site mindset.

We grew up around tradies — parents on tools, weekends spent on building sites, kids learning that hands-on work shapes the world around them. Then we became parents and watched our own little ones reach for the iPad before the dirt pile.

Little Tradies Cafe is our answer. A warm, neighbourhood cafe with proper coffee for the grown-ups, generous food for the table, and a construction-themed play zone where kids can dig, climb, hammer and build for hours — no screens required.

How we got here

A short build log.

2023

An idea on a napkin

Two parents, one playground, way too many soggy chips. We sketched what a better Saturday looked like.

Early 2024

Found our worksite

A converted warehouse on Little Builders Lane — big enough for a coffee bench, a dig pit and a hundred hi-vis vests.

Late 2024

Hard hats on

Doors opened. First flat white poured at 6:58am. First sandpit casualty at 8:15.

Today

Built by the locals

Weekly workshops. Tradie of the Month. A regular crew of families who feel like co-builders.

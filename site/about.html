Our Story

A Café Built with a Tradie Mindset.

We grew up around tradies — parents on tools, weekends spent on building sites, kids learning that hands-on work shapes the world around them. Then we became parents and watched our own little ones reach for the iPad before the dirt pile.

Little Tradies Café is our answer—a warm, neighbourhood café with proper coffee for the grown-ups, generous food for the table, and a construction-themed play zone where kids can dig, imagine, hammer and build for hours.

With a sandpit, building activities and our signature logo wall, it is hands-on fun with no screens required—or allowed.

Play hard. Build big. Sip slow.

What we stand for

The four pillars of every visit.

Built for play. Brewed for parents.

Parent Friendly

Clean tables, plenty of pram parking, and proper coffee that doesn't taste like it was made in a panic—or by your child.

Imaginative Play

A real sandpit for little hands, complete with digging toys, mini cranes, trucks, excavators, high-vis dress-ups and maybe a tradie.

Great Coffee & Food

Premium Melbourne-roasted Axil coffee. Honest cooking. An affordable menu built for young families, with fun food for kids and simple, satisfying meals for parents—served hot and still hot when you finally get to eat.

Local & Welcoming

We back the tradies, suppliers and families on our doorstep. This community helped build Little Tradies Café, so each month we spotlight a local trade business, help connect them with new customers and shout their team free coffee.

How we got here

A short build log.

2025

An idea NOT on a napkin

Two parents, one freezing outdoor playground, and coffee and food far, far away. We wanted to recreate the family-friendly spaces we loved on the Central Coast, along with the backyard adventures we grew up with as kids.

Early 2026

Found our worksite

We converted an old café warehouse on Forsyth Street overlooking the lagoon (please don't feed the ducks) — big enough for a coffee takeaway bench for early morning tradies racing to get to the job site on time, a proper sandpit and plenty of hi-vis vests, including pink and purple for some of the hardest-workers in our families.

Late 2026

Hard hats on

Doors opened. First flat white poured at 6:00am. First sandpit dispute broke out at 8:15am. Important lesson: sharing the truck is part of the job.

Today

Built by the locals

An amazing community, weekly stories of job sites that did not quite go to plan — both real and imagined — and our Tradie of the Month. Add a regular crew of brilliant staff and families who feel more like co-builders than customers.

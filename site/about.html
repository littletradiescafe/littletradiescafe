Our Story

Built by tradies.
Run by parents.

We opened Little Tradies Cafe to celebrate the builders, sparkies, and chippies who built our community. A place where kids play, parents relax, and we all remember who keeps our streets standing.

Why we're here

Coffee for the tradies

A real espresso for the tradies who built our site. Specialty milk, single origins, house-made sausage rolls. The good stuff.

Play for the kids

A safe, imaginative space where kids can build, dig, and create. Themed around the worksite — because kids love tools as much as parents do.

Community for everyone

Monthly celebrations of local tradies. Workshops teaching real skills. Events that bring families together. Because the best communities are built on connection.

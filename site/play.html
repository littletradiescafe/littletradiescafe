Site rules

Safe site, happy site.

A short list so everyone has a good time.

1

Parents stay on-site. The play zone is parent-supervised — we're a cafe, not a creche.

2

Socks required. Bring a pair, or buy a fresh set at the bench ($4).

3

Outside food & drink stay outside. Cakes for birthday bookings only, please.

4

No food in the zones. Snack at the table — sticky hands meet sand otherwise.

5

Big kids slow down. A lot of the crew are under 5 — please look out for them.

6

Pack-up rings. 4:45pm, we ring the bell, hi-vis vests back on the wall, please.

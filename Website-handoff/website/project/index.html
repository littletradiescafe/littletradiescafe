What you'll find inside

A whole worksite for little hands.

Coffee for the grown-ups. Confidence for the kids.

Dress-Up & Dig

Hi-vis vests, hard hats and a sandpit dig site. Pretend-play stations let kids haul, dump and build.

Proper Coffee

Local roast, milk on tap, and a bench wide enough for the pram. Babycinos always come with a marshmallow.

Hearty Food

Brekkie rolls, builder-sized lunches, and a kids menu of nuggets, mini pizzas and snack boxes.

Mini Workshops

Weekend make-and-take sessions hosted by real tradies — hammering, sawing (foam), painting and more.

Birthday Builds

Theme parties with hard-hat décor, mini cement-mixer cakes and a private play hour for your crew.

Tradie of the Month

We celebrate a local builder, sparkie or chippie every month — on the wall, on the house, on the socials.

Frequently Asked

Before you swing by.

Do we need to book?

Not for groups of 5 or fewer — just walk in. For 6+ or for a birthday build, please use the form above so we can hold a table.

What's the play zone age range?

The zones are designed for 2–10 year olds. Under-2s can pop in for free with a paying sibling — parents stay on site at all times.

Is there parking?

Yes — 20 free spots out the front, plus a pram-friendly drop-off lane. Bike racks under the awning.

Can we bring our own cake?

Only for confirmed birthday-build bookings (cakeage waived). Otherwise outside food & drink stay outside, please.

Do you cater to allergies?

We've got veggie, vegan and gluten-friendly options on the regular menu. For severe allergies (especially nuts), drop us a line ahead of time so we can prep safely.

Are dogs allowed?

Friendly dogs are welcome at the outdoor benches. The indoor cafe and play zone are a no-dog zone for hygiene and allergy reasons.

What does Tradie of the Month get?

A coffee on the house every day for the month, their photo and story on the wall, a feature in the newsletter, and a tagged shout on our socials.
